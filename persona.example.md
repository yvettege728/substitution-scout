# Persona card

The only file that may be shown to another agent. It is a summary by layer. It
carries no names, no addresses, no biography and no story.

Everything in profile.md stays private unless the person approves a specific
disclosure, one disclosure at a time.

## Summary

- Moved between cities. Earlier city, current city.
- Layer 1 to 3 (material, sign, economic): open to suggestions from anyone.
- Layer 4 (brand and category habits): ask people with similar habits.
- Layer 5 (what brings order back): weight given to routine, to discovering new
  products, and to talking about finds with other people.

## Disclosure rule

Deeper layers travel in narrower circles. Layer 1 to 3 questions can go to
strangers. Layer 4 questions go to people with similar habits. Layer 5 questions
go only where the person has approved sharing a detail, and each detail is
approved separately.

When a disclosure would help, propose it to the person in this form:
"To ask X, I would tell them Y about you. Shall I?" Then wait.
