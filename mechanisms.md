# Five mechanisms of substitution

These came out of real substitutions recorded after one move between countries.
They are the ground truth the agent reasons against. The private repository holds
the cases themselves; what is portable is the shape of each mechanism.

| Mechanism | What happened | What the agent should look for |
|---|---|---|
| Category jump | a biscuit was replaced by a chocolate bark | the occasion, not the product. When the ritual is a reward, material and sign similarity are close to irrelevant |
| Self-assembly | a packaged cereal was replaced by mixing one at a bulk dispenser | whether the components exist, and whether composing would be welcome. The substitute can be a practice, and it can end up preferred |
| Unintended switch | a brand was present but in a different pack, so it went unrecognised and lost the customer | whether the original is here under a different look. Recognition is a precondition for loyalty, not a consequence of it |
| Split | one drink served both coffee and an evening comfort ritual, and the two needed different answers | whether one item is serving two occasions. Searching for a single substitute while two cells are open produces a candidate that satisfies neither |
| Lexical miss | the person searched their own word for the product; the local market uses another | whether the thing exists here under a different word. An agent that searches only the person's vocabulary inherits the person's blind spot |

An agent's first job on a new item is to say which of these is in play. Check the
unintended switch before accepting any substitution the person already made, and
run the split test before searching at all.

## The two axes

**Property layers** say what a thing is: material, sign, economic, relational,
order and trust.

**Ritual layers** say which step of the practice broke: search and discovery,
acquisition, possession, use, sharing and telling, divestment.

They are perpendicular. The cell, not the item, is the unit of substitution. Which
cell decides a given item is unknown when a run begins, which is why the sequence
of actions cannot be written in advance.
